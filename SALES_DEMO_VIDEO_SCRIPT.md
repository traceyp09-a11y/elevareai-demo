## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Visual Style Guide
- **Color Palette:** Dark backgrounds (#0f172a), cyan highlights (#06b6d4), blue accents (#3b82f6)
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, or similar)
- **Animation Style:** Smooth, professional transitions. No flashy effects.
- **Screen Recordings:** Use actual platform footage from localhost:3000
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps minimum, 60fps preferred for smooth UI captures

### Screen Recording Checklist
- ✅ HR Dashboard → Custom Reports
- ✅ HSE Dashboard → Safety metrics
- ✅ Operations Dashboard → Production KPIs
- ✅ Finance Dashboard → Financial metrics
- ✅ Custom Reports → All 3 templates per department
- ✅ Export functionality → Word, Excel, PDF
- ✅ Real-time data updates
- ✅ 12-month trend charts

### Voiceover Direction
- **Tone:** Authoritative but approachable. Think McKinsey presentation, not infomercial.
- **Pacing:** Measured and confident. Pause for emphasis on key statistics.
- **Gender:** Male or female—choose voice that conveys executive credibility
- **Accent:** Neutral American or British English

### Music Selection
- **Opening (Problem):** Subtle tension, minor key, atmospheric
- **Reveal:** Shift to modern, confident corporate track
- **Demo Sections:** Upbeat but professional, not distracting
- **Closing:** Triumphant, inspiring crescendo

### B-Roll Suggestions
- Modern office environments
- C-suite executives in meetings
- Manufacturing floor operations
- Data centers (representing the tech backbone)
- Boardroom presentations
- Hands using tablets/laptops confidently
